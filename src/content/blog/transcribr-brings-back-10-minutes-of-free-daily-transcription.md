# Transcribr Brings Back 10 Minutes of Free Daily Transcription

---

## We Heard You

Over the past few weeks, we received a steady stream of emails and messages from Transcribr users asking us to restore the free daily transcription we used to offer.

Many of you told us how much it mattered — especially for students, researchers, professionals, and content creators who rely on transcription but aren't ready to commit to a paid plan.

We listened. **10 minutes of free daily transcription is back on Transcribr.**

## What's Changing

Starting now, you can transcribe **up to 10 minutes of audio or video every day** at no cost — no subscription, no credit card, no catch.

Interviews, lectures, meetings, podcasts, voice memos — upload it, and Transcribr converts it into text. If you need more than 10 minutes a day, our paid plans offer extra capacity and advanced features for heavier use. But you're never required to pay just to get started.

## Why We Brought It Back

We don't make product decisions in a vacuum.

When we scaled back the free tier, many of you explained exactly what it meant to lose it: help with schoolwork and research, a way to keep working while saving up for a subscription, a tool you depended on more than we realized.

That feedback stuck with us. A feature that looks small on a roadmap can matter enormously to the people actually using it — so after weighing it carefully, we brought it back.

## Built for Where You're At

Transcription should be accessible no matter where you are in your work:

- A **student** transcribing lecture recordings
- A **researcher** working through interviews
- A **journalist** turning tape into copy
- A **professional** converting meeting notes into text

Whatever your reason, you can start using Transcribr today without committing to anything.

## We're Listening

This is about more than restoring a feature — it's about the kind of company we want to be. We want to understand how people actually use Transcribr and make decisions that balance sustainability with accessibility.

Your feedback changed our course, and we're grateful for it. Thank you for using Transcribr, and thank you for speaking up.

Upload your audio or video and let Transcribr turn it into text — free, every day, no setup required.
