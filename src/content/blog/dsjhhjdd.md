Price a show before you commit to it
Paste the address and press Estimate. Transcribr reads the feed and tells you what watching it would actually use: how many episodes the show publishes in a month, the average episode length, and the audio hours those add up to. The numbers come from the show’s own publishing history rather than an assumed weekly hour, and the estimate says plainly whether that fits inside the hours you already have.

Some feeds do not publish episode lengths. When that happens the estimate tells you it had to assume, instead of quietly presenting a guess as a measurement.
